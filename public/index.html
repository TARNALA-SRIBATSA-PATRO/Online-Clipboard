Frequently Asked Questions

        

          

            How does Online Clipboard work?
            

              
Simply type or paste text into the composer, attach optional files, and click Create Clipboard. You receive a unique 6-digit retrieval code, direct link, and QR code to access your content instantly on any other device.

            

          

          

            Is it really 100% free with no login or signup required?
            

              
Yes. You will never be asked for an email address, phone number, password, or credit card. Online Clipboard is completely free and requires zero user registration.

            

          

          

            Are my notes and files secure?
            

              
Yes. You can optionally specify a passcode for sensitive payloads. Furthermore, all clips are ephemeral—they automatically self-destruct after your selected lifespan (from 1 minute to 30 minutes) or immediately upon reading if Burn After Reading is toggled.

            

          

          

            Can I use this without seeing advertisements?
            

              
Yes. Online Clipboard is built with zero banner ads, zero popups, and zero third-party commercial trackers to guarantee top speed and privacy.

            

          

          

            Can I install Online Clipboard on my mobile home screen?
            

              
Yes. Online Clipboard is a Progressive Web App (PWA). On mobile, tap the + Home shortcut button to add it directly to your home screen for one-tap native app access.
